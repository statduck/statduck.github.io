I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, where I began my doctoral studies in August 2026.

My research interests lie in **causal inference**, **structural causal models**, and **probabilistic machine learning**. I am interested in understanding how causal mechanisms change and in building methods that remain reliable across different environments.

I received my M.S. in Statistics from Yonsei University in February 2026, where I conducted research under the supervision of Prof. Kyungwoo Song, and my B.A. in Applied Statistics from Yonsei University in August 2022. Before graduate school, I worked as a Data Analyst at **NAVER FINANCIAL**.

## Research

- **Unmeasured confounding:** I am interested in methods for causal inference in the presence of unmeasured confounding, with a particular focus on proximal causal inference.
- **Causal mechanism shifts:** I developed FANS (Function And Noise Separation), which uses normalizing flows to distinguish changes in causal functions from changes in noise distributions. This work appeared at ICML 2026.
- **Robust machine learning:** My collaborative work explores black-box visual prompting and causal-aware in-context learning for out-of-distribution generalization.
